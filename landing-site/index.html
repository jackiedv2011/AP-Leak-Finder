Introducing
Reclaim.

A recovery system with an audit layer built in, not a dashboard that lists what you lost.

Reclaim starts with the records you already have: accounting exports, bill lists, payment histories and vendor-credit files. It audits them for focused, high-confidence opportunities and turns each supported finding into a structured case: what happened, how much is involved, what evidence supports it and what is still missing.

Reclaim assesses how recoverable each case is, recommends the next step and pursues it with your team through to resolution. Every claim is reviewable, and no message reaches a vendor until you approve it. Not just money found. Money followed through.
