The Recovery Ledger

Billed at the last step, not the first

Found money and recovered money are different numbers, and Reclaim never blurs them.
Every case moves through the same five stages, and the Recovery Ledger records the amount at each one. The Autopilot rate applies to the last stage only: cash that settled, or a credit that actually offset a real bill. Nothing earlier is billable.
  1. 01
    Discover

    A signal turns into one case: same vendor, same obligation, both payments settled.

  2. 02
    Verify

    The records are checked against each other until the amount is provable, or marked as needing review.

  3. 03
    Recover

    You approve it. The playbook builds the evidence, contacts the vendor, and tracks every reply.

  4. 04
    Reconcile

    The refund or credit is confirmed against your books, and the accounting is closed out.

  5. 05
    Recovered

    Money is back. This is the only figure the Autopilot rate is calculated on.

FAQ

  • What counts as recovered?

    Cash that settled, or a credit that actually offset a real bill. A vendor agreeing that they owe you is not recovered. A credit note sitting unapplied is not recovered either — it is tracked as confirmed but unapplied until it is used. The Autopilot rate applies only once value has genuinely returned.

  • Which plan do I need?

    Plans are sized on annual vendor spend, not headcount or transaction count: $5M for Core, $30M for Continuous, $100M for Scale. If your spend grows past a tier mid-term, you move up at renewal rather than being cut off.

  • Does the Autopilot rate apply to everything Reclaim finds?

    No. It applies to recoveries Reclaim caused. Every case records how it was discovered, when, and whether you had already flagged it, so an issue your team was already working is not billed as ours.

  • What about a credit we already had?

    An unused vendor credit is money you already own, so recovering it needs no outreach at all — Reclaim just shows you the credit and the bill it should be applied against. It counts once the credit is actually used.

  • Can Reclaim move money or change our books?

    No. The accounting connection is read-only. Reclaim cannot move money, cannot alter your ledger, and cannot send anything to a vendor without your approval. Refunds go directly to you; Reclaim never takes custody of recovered funds.

  • Will you contact our vendors without asking?

    Not unless you set that rule yourself. The first outreach on any case needs your approval. You can later let approved playbooks run automatically within limits you define, and you can mark any vendor Do Not Contact — detection keeps running, outreach stops.

  • What happens to a disputed case?

    Automation stops and the case comes back to you. Anything subjective, contested, or legal is a decision for your team, not a step a playbook takes on its own.

  • Is there a setup fee or a contract?

    No setup fee, and you can cancel anytime. Annual billing takes two months off. Onboarding is self-service: connect the accounting system, pick the lookback period, and the first sweep starts.
