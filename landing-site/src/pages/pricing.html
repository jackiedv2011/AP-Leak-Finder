The Recovery Ledger

Counted when it's back, not when it's found

Found money and recovered money are different numbers, and Reclaim never blurs them.
Every case moves through the same stages, and the Recovery Ledger records the amount at each one, along with how the issue was discovered and whether your team already knew about it. Only one stage counts as recovered: cash that settled, or a credit that actually offset a real bill.
  1. 01
    Discover

    Related signals become one case: same vendor, same obligation, both payments settled.

  2. 02
    Verify

    The records are checked against each other until the amount is provable, or marked as needing review.

  3. 03
    Recover

    You approve it. Reclaim sends the request with the evidence, tracks every reply, and follows up.

  4. 04
    Recovered

    The refund settled, or the credit offset a real bill. This is the only figure Reclaim reports as recovered.

  5. 05
    Reconcile

    Reclaim gives you the accounting steps to close the case in your books, and you confirm them.

FAQ

  • How much does Reclaim cost?

    Free costs nothing and shows the three smallest findings in each upload. Growth is $15 a month plus 10% of money that actually comes back. Flat is $50 a month with no success fee. Paid plans open with our supervised pilots; talk to us to join one. Refunds and credits always go directly to you.

  • What does the free audit include?

    Upload an export and Reclaim runs every check on it at no cost. On the free plan, three of the findings are shown in full, with the records behind them. The full list comes with a paid plan.

  • What counts as recovered?

    Cash that settled, or a credit that actually offset a real bill. A vendor agreeing that they owe you is not recovered. A credit note sitting unapplied is not recovered either: it is tracked as confirmed but unapplied until it is used.

  • Which part of the price depends on what's recovered?

    Only Growth's 10%, and only on cash that settled or a credit that actually offset a real bill. It is worked out from the Recovery Ledger, which records for every case how the issue was discovered, when, and whether your team already knew about it. A finding, a vendor's promise or an unused credit is never charged. On Flat, nothing depends on recovery.

  • What about a credit we already had?

    An unused vendor credit is money you already own, so recovering it may need no outreach at all. Reclaim shows you the credit and recommends whether to apply it to an upcoming bill or ask for a cash refund. It counts once the credit is actually used or the refund arrives.

  • Can Reclaim move money or change our books?

    No. Reclaim works from files you upload and has no access to your accounting system. It cannot move money, cannot alter your ledger, and cannot send anything to a vendor without your approval. Refunds go directly to you; Reclaim never takes custody of recovered funds.

  • Will you contact our vendors without asking?

    No. The first outreach on any case needs your approval, and you can mark any vendor Do Not Contact: detection keeps running, outreach stops. Later, we plan to let you choose rules under which approved playbooks start on their own, such as exact duplicates under an amount you set. That mode, Recovery Autopilot, is software you configure, not a person working your cases, and it isn't available yet.

  • What happens to a disputed case?

    Automation stops and the case comes back to you. Anything subjective, contested, or legal is a decision for your team, not a step a playbook takes on its own.

  • How do we get started?

    Onboarding is self-service: upload your exports, choose how far back to look, and the first sweep starts. Billing and cancelling happen in the product too, with no call needed.
