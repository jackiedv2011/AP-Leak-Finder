The Recovery Ledger

Billed at the last step, not the first

Found money and recovered money are different numbers, and Reclaim never blurs them.
Every case moves through the same five stages, and the Recovery Ledger records the amount at each one. Growth’s 15% applies to the last stage only: cash that settled, or a credit that actually offset a real bill. Nothing earlier is billable, and Flat never charges a fee at all.
  1. 01
    Discover

    A signal turns into one case: same vendor, same obligation, both payments settled.

  2. 02
    Verify

    The records are checked against each other until the amount is provable, or marked as needing review.

  3. 03
    Recover

    You approve it. Reclaim drafts the request from the records and tracks every reply; you send it from your own email.

  4. 04
    Reconcile

    The refund or credit is confirmed against your books, and the accounting is closed out.

  5. 05
    Recovered

    Money is back. This is the only figure Growth’s success fee is calculated on.

FAQ

  • What does Free actually include?

    Unlimited uploads, and every check runs on every file. For each ledger you upload you see the three lowest-value findings in full, with their evidence and a recovery letter you can copy. The larger findings are listed but locked until you move to Growth or Flat.

  • Can I upload the same file again to see other findings?

    No. On Free each ledger is audited once. Reclaim recognizes a repeat by its payments, not its file name, so a renamed copy or a smaller piece of a file you already uploaded is flagged and doesn’t unlock anything new. A new month’s export is a new ledger and is welcome.

  • What counts as recovered?

    Cash that settled, or a credit that actually offset a real bill. A vendor agreeing that they owe you is not recovered, and neither is a credit note sitting unapplied. Growth’s 15% applies only once value has genuinely come back.

  • Growth or Flat?

    Same features. Growth costs $19.99 a month plus 15% of what’s recovered; Flat is $100 a month with no fee. If you expect to recover more than $533.40 a month, Flat costs less.

  • Do you email our vendors for us?

    Reclaim drafts every recovery email — with AI on Growth and Flat — and you send it from your own inbox. Automatic sending through your Gmail is coming soon, and even then nothing goes to a vendor without your approval.

  • Can Reclaim move money or change our books?

    No. You upload a CSV export; Reclaim never connects to your bank or accounting system, cannot move money, and never takes custody of recovered funds.

  • Is there a setup fee or a contract?

    No setup fee, no contract, cancel anytime. Paid plans open soon — nothing is charged today.
