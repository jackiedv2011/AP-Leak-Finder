You choose
what we see

Reclaim works from files you export and upload. No connection to your accounting system, no credentials, no way to move money.

Talk to us

What Reclaim reads

  • The files you choose
    You export the records you want checked and upload them. Nothing else comes with them.
  • No system access
    Reclaim doesn't connect to your accounting system and never asks for a login.
  • Read, never write
    Nothing in your books changes. Reclaim reads the exports and reports what it finds.
  • No payment rights
    Reclaim cannot move money, release a payment, or contact a vendor.

Every result points
at a row

  • Traced to a file
    Every finding names the file it came from, the row inside it, and the fields that matched.
  • Shown side by side
    The bill and the payment sit side by side with the differences marked, not as a figure you have to trust.
  • Yours to check
    You can open the export you uploaded and check any result against the original rows.
  • Yours to decide
    Reclaim doesn't act on anything it finds. What happens next is your decision, not its.

What we
never ask for

Three things Reclaim has no use for, and does not request.

  • Logins
    Reclaim never needs credentials for your bank, your accounting system or a vendor portal. It never asks, and there is nowhere in the product to enter them.
  • Payment authority
    Nothing in Reclaim can approve, release or reverse a payment. Whoever holds that authority today still holds it afterwards.
  • Your whole ledger
    You pick the reports and the date range. Anything outside what you exported never reaches Reclaim at all.

What we'll publish
before launch

Some of what belongs on a security page depends on decisions we are still making about storage.

Where an uploaded file is held, how it is encrypted, how long it is kept and how it is removed are not settled yet. We would rather leave that blank than write something we might have to correct later. It will be published here before Reclaim opens to customers.

Found
something?

If you think you have found a security problem with this site or with Reclaim, tell us before you tell anyone else. Use the contact form and say that it is a security report.

We don't run a bug bounty programme, and we would rather say that plainly than imply one exists.
