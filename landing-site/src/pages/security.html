You choose
what we see

Connect your accounting system with read-only access, or upload exports instead. Either way, Reclaim can't move money, can't change your books, and contacts no vendor until you approve it.

Talk to us

What Reclaim reads

  • The source you choose
    Connect QuickBooks Online or Xero with the narrowest read access they offer, or export the records you want checked and upload them instead.
  • Yours to revoke
    A connection can be removed whenever you like. You can also export or delete your data.
  • Read, never write
    Nothing in your books changes. Reclaim reads your records, and when a case is closed it gives you the accounting steps to make yourself.
  • No payment rights
    Reclaim cannot move money or release a payment. Refunds and credits go straight to you; Reclaim never holds recovered funds.

Every result points
at a record

  • Traced to the source
    Every finding names the records it rests on, where each one came from in your books or your file, and the fields that matched.
  • Shown side by side
    The bill and the payment sit side by side with the differences marked, not as a figure you have to trust.
  • Yours to check
    You can open any record behind a result and check it against your books or the export you uploaded.
  • Yours to decide
    Nothing goes to a vendor until you approve the recovery, and every step taken after that is logged: who, what, when and why.

What Reclaim
never gets

Three things Reclaim has no use for, and is built never to have.

  • Your passwords
    Connecting QuickBooks Online or Xero happens on their own sign-in and approval screen. Reclaim never asks for your password to your accounting system, your bank or a vendor portal.
  • Payment authority
    Nothing in Reclaim can approve, release or reverse a payment. Whoever holds that authority today still holds it afterwards.
  • Your ledger, in an AI prompt
    AI never browses your books. The financial engine works out the numbers; AI gets only the few facts one task needs, like the vendor, invoice and amount for a single email.

What we'll publish
before launch

Some of what belongs on a security page depends on decisions we are still making about storage.

Reclaim is built to keep each customer's data separate, encrypt your financial records and connection tokens, and let you export or delete what it holds. Where that data is kept, how long, and exactly which standards protect it are not settled yet. We would rather leave that blank than write something we might have to correct later. It will be published here before Reclaim opens to customers.

Found

If you think you have found a security problem with this site or with Reclaim, tell us before you tell anyone else. Use the contact form and say that it is a security report.

We don't run a bug bounty programme, and we would rather say that plainly than imply one exists.
