Only what the records show

Most payment mistakes aren't hidden. They're buried: two payments for one invoice, or a credit that was never applied.
Reclaim checks every payment against the bills, invoices, and credits around it. It looks for duplicate payments, duplicate bills, overpayments, unused credits, and payments that don't match what was billed. You get a short list of findings, not a long list of maybes, each with the amount involved.
