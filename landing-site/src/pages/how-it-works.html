Start with what you have

The records that show a payment mistake already exist, spread across your accounting system, your bank, and a few spreadsheets.
Export them the way you normally would and add them to Reclaim: bills, invoices, payment history, vendor credits. Reclaim lines the files up against each other, so a payment in one export can be matched to a bill in another. No integration project, and no new process for your team.

Only what the records show

Most payment mistakes aren't hidden. They're buried: two payments for one invoice, or a credit that was never applied.
Reclaim checks every payment against the bills, invoices, and credits around it. It looks for duplicate payments, duplicate bills, overpayments, unused credits, and payments that don't match what was billed. You get a short list of findings, not a long list of maybes, each with the amount involved.

Evidence

Proof behind every finding

A finding is only useful if you can check it. Every result in Reclaim comes with the records behind it.
Open a finding and see the bill, the payments, the dates, and the amounts, with the details that match called out. Each record points back to the file and row it came from. You decide whether it's a real mistake, so a finding never rests on a guess.
