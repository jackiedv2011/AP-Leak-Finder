Exporting from NetSuite

NetSuite can produce exactly what Reclaim needs, either from the standard payables reports or from four saved searches.

What Reclaim reads

Reclaim works from four exports out of NetSuite: your bills, the payments that went against them, your vendor list, and any credits. Nothing is connected to your account, and nothing is changed inside it. You export the files, you upload them, and you decide what to do with what comes back.

The four files do different jobs, and the comparison only works when all four are there. A duplicate payment is invisible in the bill file alone; an unused credit is invisible in the payment file alone.

Files

What to export

  • Transaction Detail (bills)
  • Transaction Detail (payments)
  • Vendor list
  • Open vendor credits

Purpose

What each one does

  • Bills give every invoice its vendor, number, date and amount
  • Payments show what actually left, and against which bill
  • Vendors let two spellings of one supplier be recognised
  • Credits show money already owed back to you

Reading time about four minutes · Export time about fifteen

Before you begin

What you need

A NetSuite login with permission to run and export reports.

A date range in mind — two years is a good first pass.

Somewhere to put four files while you work.

Export permission is separate

In most systems, being able to see a report and being able to export it are two different permissions. If the export control is missing rather than greyed out, it is a permission question for whoever administers your account, not a problem with the report.

01

Decide: reports or saved searches

NetSuite gives you two routes to the same files. The standard payables reports under Reports are quicker. A saved search under Lists → Search gives you exact control over the columns, which is worth it if your account has heavy customisation.

Either works. If you already have saved searches your team uses for payables, start from those — the columns are usually right already.

# the two routes
Reports → Vendors/Payables → Transaction Detail
Lists → Search → Saved Searches → New

One subsidiary at a time

In OneWorld accounts, export per subsidiary unless you genuinely want them compared together. Intercompany activity across subsidiaries can read as duplication when it is nothing of the kind.

02

Set the date range and filters

Set the period on the report footer or in the search criteria, and use the same range for every export.

Filter to posting transactions. Reclaim is comparing money that moved against bills that were raised, so memorised transactions, templates and unapproved records add noise without adding information.

Two years, accrual

A two-year window catches the duplicates that sit months apart. Keep the basis on accrual so unpaid bills stay in the population.

Watch the row limit

NetSuite exports can be capped by your account settings. If a file comes out suspiciously round — exactly 10,000 rows, say — split the range in half and export twice.

03

Export your bills

Run the transaction detail report filtered to Vendor Bill, or run a transaction saved search with the same type filter.

Use the CSV export control on the report or search results. Include the internal ID column if it is available: it is the cleanest way for Reclaim to point a result back at a specific record in your account.

04

Export your payments

Repeat the export with the type filter set to Bill Payment, and include the applied-to document so each payment carries the bill it settled.

That link is the whole point. A payment with no applied bill, or two payments applied to the same bill, is the shape of almost everything Reclaim reports.

Include every payment method

Checks, ACH, wires and credit card payments all sit in the same transaction type family. Filtering to one method is the most common reason a duplicate stays hidden.

05

Export your vendor list

Go to Lists → Relationships → Vendors and export the list.

Duplicate vendor records are unusually common in NetSuite because the same supplier can be created separately in different subsidiaries or by different teams. Each copy carries its own bills, and nothing compares them until something does.

Include inactive vendors

Inactive records still hold history. A vendor deactivated after a merge is exactly the record that explains why two bills never got matched.

06

Export open credits

Export Vendor Credit transactions with their applied and unapplied amounts, either from the payables reports or from a saved search.

What you want is the remaining balance per credit, not just the original amount. A credit that has been half applied is still money sitting on the account.

07

Check the files before you upload

Open each CSV once. Three things, a minute in total.

That the header row is the header and not a report title. That amounts are numbers rather than text with currency symbols baked in. And that the row count looks like the volume you expected rather than a truncated export.

Send them unedited

Every result Reclaim reports points at a row in the file you uploaded. Cleaning the file up first breaks that link, which is the part that makes a finding checkable.

Troubleshooting

I don't have permission to export

Export permissions in NetSuite are role-based, and reporting roles often have them where data-entry roles do not. Your administrator can grant export on the specific reports or searches rather than opening anything broader.

Which is better, a report or a saved search?

A saved search, if your account is customised. It gives you the exact columns, it reruns identically next time, and it is easier to hand to someone else. The standard reports are faster for a first pass.

My export is missing the applied document

Add the applying transaction column to the search, or export the bill payment application detail alongside it. Without that link Reclaim can still match on amount and vendor, but with it the evidence is much stronger.

Do I need to export journals too?

Not for a first run. Bills, payments, vendors and credits cover the population. Journals matter when a specific finding needs explaining, and you can pull those individually at that point.

Can Reclaim connect to NetSuite directly instead?

Not today. Reclaim works from exports you upload. That keeps it out of your account entirely, which is usually the easier conversation with whoever owns NetSuite security.
