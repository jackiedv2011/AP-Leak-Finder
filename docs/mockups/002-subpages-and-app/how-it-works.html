01 · Connect

Start with what you have.

Reclaim lines the records up against each other, so a payment can be matched to the bill it paid. No integration project, and no new process for your team.

02 · Find

Only what the records show.

Most payment mistakes aren't hidden. They're buried: two payments for one invoice, or a credit that was never applied.

Reclaim checks every payment against the bills, invoices, and credits around it. It looks for duplicate payments, duplicate bills, overpayments, unused credits, and payments that don't match what was billed. You get a short list of findings, not a long list of maybes, each with the amount involved and how strong the evidence is.

03 · Evidence

Proof behind every finding.

A finding is only useful if you can check it. Every result in Reclaim comes with the records behind it.

Open a finding and see the bill, the payments, the dates, and the amounts, with the details that match called out and a plain explanation of why it was flagged. Each record points back to where it came from: the entry in your books, or the file and row you uploaded. You decide whether it's a real mistake, so a finding never rests on a guess.

04 · Recover

Followed through to the money.

Nothing goes to a vendor until you approve the recovery. After that, Reclaim does the chasing.

Approve a case and Reclaim sends the request with the evidence attached, tracks the vendor's reply, and follows up on schedule. A dispute, a partial offer or anything unclear comes back to you. A promised refund stays promised until it settles: Reclaim looks for the refund or the applied credit in your books before it counts anything as recovered, then gives you the steps to reconcile it.

FAQ

Not sure where to start? Talk to us

  • What records does Reclaim need?

    Your bills, the payments against them, your vendors, and your vendor credits. You upload them as CSV exports. Our export guides show where to find them in common accounting tools.

  • Do I need to connect my accounting system?

    No. You can start with files you export yourself. Direct connections are in development. Nothing needs to be installed.

  • What does Reclaim look for?

    Duplicate payments, duplicate bills, overpayments, unused vendor credits, and payments that don't match what was billed. Each one has its own section on What It Checks, with examples.

  • How does Reclaim decide something is a mistake?

    It compares records against each other: vendors, amounts, invoice numbers, dates, and references. A finding is raised when the records themselves show the problem, and every finding lists the records it is based on.

  • Can a finding turn out to be fine?

    Yes. A partial payment, a deposit, or a credit applied outside your export can look like a mistake. That's why every finding shows its records, and why you review it before treating it as one.

  • Which accounting tools does it work with?

    Reclaim works from exported records, so it isn't tied to one tool, and direct connections are in development. We have export guides for QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, and Bill.

  • Does Reclaim contact my vendors?

    Only once you approve a recovery. Reclaim then sends the request with the evidence attached, tracks the reply and follows up. A dispute, or anything unclear, comes back to you, and you can mark any vendor Do Not Contact.

  • How is my data handled?

    Our Security page covers what Reclaim reads, what it can never do, and how AI is kept to the minimum each task needs.

  • Who is Reclaim for?

    Businesses that pay a lot of vendors, without a team to chase every mistake: finance and AP teams, operators, and founders.
