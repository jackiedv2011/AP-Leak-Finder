    Benchmark · APQC · Ardent Partners

    How often payments go out wrong

    APQC puts duplicate or erroneous payments at 1.5% of disbursements. Ardent puts invoice exceptions at 18.4%.

  • Piece to render · an invoice slab, a coral copy sliding out

    Report · ACFE

    What 2,402 fraud cases show

    ACFE's 2026 study found billing schemes in 21% of cases, with a typical case running 12 months before anyone caught it.

  • Piece to render · an envelope in a white tray

    Survey · AFP · FBI IC3

    Payments fraud in 2025

    76% of organizations faced attempted or actual payments fraud, per AFP. Checks were the most targeted method by a wide margin.

  • Piece to render · a column of bars, a stepped shadow

    Report · GAO

    $186 billion in improper payments

    GAO counted about $186 billion in improper federal payments in fiscal 2025. About $153 billion of that was overpayments.
