The app, on the landing’s paper and ink.

Today’s dashboard, Launch and Onboarding next to the same screens with two override sheets injected into the running app. Only tokens and type change in the dashboard; Launch and Onboarding also lose the old black-clay 3D for the landing’s exhibit tiles.

  1. 01

    Warm light, ink dark.

    Light moves from cool greys to the landing’s paper (#f4f1ea canvas, #fcfbf7 cards). Dark moves from true black to its green-black ink (#08100c, #121a16).

  2. 02

    Schibsted for display.

    Page titles, display headings and the headline money switch to Schibsted Grotesk. Launch and Onboarding drop Barlow Condensed capitals for sentence case.

  3. 03

    The new 3D family.

    The black cubes and cream paper loops are hidden. Each art slot is a pastel exhibit tile with a flat placeholder of the piece still to be rendered.
